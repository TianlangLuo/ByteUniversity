Further Exploring — Beyond the Spec
Documentaries, articles, and research that bring the topic to life. Not required for the exam, but genuinely fascinating.
🌿 Environment & E-Waste
🎥 Documentary
Recycling E-Waste: Good for Business & the Environment
DW Documentary explores companies renting devices instead of selling, plus a startup recovering materials from solar modules. Shows the right-to-repair movement in action.
YouTube · DW Documentary
🎥 Documentary
How the Rich World's Electronic Waste Affects Poor Countries
ENDEVR investigative documentary exposing how discarded electronics from wealthy nations end up in African scrapyards, with devastating consequences for local communities.
YouTube · ENDEVR
🎥 Documentary
The Digital Afterlife: Unravelling the E-Waste Crisis
A full documentary on what really happens to your old phones, tablets and computers after you throw them away — from shipping containers to landfill sites.
YouTube · E-Life
👤 Personal Data & Privacy
🎥 Documentary
The Great Hack (Netflix)
The Cambridge Analytica story — how personal data harvested from millions of Facebook users was used to influence the 2016 US election and Brexit vote. Raises serious questions about data ownership and consent.
Netflix · Cambridge Analytica
💬 TED Talk
Facebook's Role in Brexit — and the Threat to Democracy
Journalist Carole Cadwalladr breaks down how personal data and targeted advertising were weaponised for political ends. A chilling real-world extension of what the DPA 2018 tries to prevent.
TED · Carole Cadwalladr
💬 TED Talk
Surveillance Capitalism — How Your Data Becomes Their Profit
Shoshana Zuboff explains how tech companies harvest behavioural data to predict and influence human behaviour. Essential context for understanding why the DPA 2018 and GDPR exist.
TED · Shoshana Zuboff
🤖 AI, Bias & Ethics
💬 TED Talk
How I'm Fighting Bias in Algorithms
MIT researcher Joy Buolamwini discovered that facial recognition systems worked far worse on darker skin tones. A vivid, accessible demonstration of algorithmic bias and why it matters.
TED · Joy Buolamwini
📄 Journal
AI as Moral Cover: How Algorithmic Bias Exploits Psychology
Peer-reviewed research showing how AI systems allow people to perpetuate discrimination while maintaining beliefs in their own objectivity — the "moral cover" phenomenon. Directly relevant to the black-box problem.
Society for Psychological Study · 2025
📄 Journal
Algorithmic Discrimination: Types and Regulatory Measures
Academic analysis of five primary types of algorithmic bias and how different legal jurisdictions are responding. Connects directly to AI ethics and the regulatory challenges of the black-box problem.
Frontiers in AI · 2024
🔓 Cyber Threats & Hacking
🎥 Documentary
Cyberattacks, Data Encryption, Extortion — How Cybercriminals Operate
DW Documentary reveals how ransomware gangs operate like professional businesses — with customer service, negotiators, and HR departments. A real-world look at the ransomware threat.
YouTube · DW Documentary
🎥 Documentary
Zero Days — Stuxnet & the World's First Cyber Weapon
Alex Gibney's acclaimed documentary about Stuxnet — the malware allegedly created by the US and Israel to sabotage Iran's nuclear programme. A real-life Offence 3 (Computer Misuse Act) on a nation-state scale.
YouTube / Alex Gibney · 2016
🎥 Documentary
Citizenfour — Edward Snowden & Mass Surveillance
Oscar-winning documentary capturing the moment Snowden revealed the NSA's global surveillance programmes. Deeply relevant to privacy, personal data, and civil liberties — and what happens when those in power exploit technical access.
YouTube / Laura Poitras · 2014
📜 Intellectual Property
💬 TED Talk
Laws That Choke Creativity
Harvard Law professor Larry Lessig argues that current copyright law is strangling creativity and culture. A compelling argument for why Creative Commons and open licensing models exist.
TED · Larry Lessig
📰 Article
Is Open Source Software "IP"? Debunking the Myth
Accessible explainer busting the myth that open source code has no intellectual property protection. Shows how copyright still applies even to freely shared code — and what happens if you violate a licence.
Scale AI · IP Without Jargon
📄 Journal
Amazon's AI Hiring Tool & Gender Discrimination
Cornell Law review of how Amazon's AI recruitment system discriminated against women because of biased training data — a perfect real-world example of algorithmic bias meeting IP and employment law.
Cornell Journal of Law · 2024

ByteUniversity

Revision that actually works.

ByteUniversity is an interactive GCSE Computer Science revision platform built to go beyond passive reading. Every feature — from card-by-card content delivery to spaced repetition scheduling to playable games — is designed around how memory actually works.

📖
Active Learning, Not Passive Reading
Content reveals one card at a time, with checkpoint questions blocking progress until each concept is genuinely understood — no skimming allowed.
📅
Spaced Repetition Built In
A smart calendar schedules exactly when to revisit each topic based on performance, using the same memory science behind tools like Anki.
⚡
XP & Avatars
Every correct answer, completed subtopic, and on-time review earns XP — spent in the avatar shop to unlock new characters, keeping motivation high throughout revision.
🏅
18 Unlockable Badges
